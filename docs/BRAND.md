# Auralis — Brand Direction

## Brand name

**Auralis**

Use this spelling consistently in the app name, documentation, package metadata where appropriate, and future publishing materials.

## Product descriptor

AI podcast player for Android.

Suggested short description:

> Listen to AI-generated podcasts, offline and on your terms.

## Visual direction

- Deep black backgrounds.
- Dark navy surfaces.
- Electric cyan/blue as the primary interactive accent.
- Violet as a secondary accent, especially in gradients and branded artwork.
- High contrast, restrained glow, clear typography, and generous spacing.
- The user-provided Auralis “A” logo is the primary brand reference.

Initial design tokens (provisional):

| Token | Value | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #08090F | Main app background |
| Surface | #10172B | Cards and panels |
| Primary | #22BFFF | Main actions and active states |
| Secondary | #A855F7 | Secondary accent |
| Text | #F4F6FF | Primary text |
| Muted text | #A4ABC4 | Secondary metadata |

These values are a starting point, not sampled exact values from the original logo file. Tune them after reviewing the actual asset in the app.

## Interface references

Poweramp is a reference for playback ergonomics and mature audio controls only. Do not copy its branding, assets, layouts, or implementation. Auralis should remain recognizable as its own AI-podcast product.

## Logo and app icon

The supplied logo image should be added to the repository as an approved source asset before generating Android launcher icon variants. Do not recreate or replace it with a generic generated mark without a deliberate design decision.

## Tone

Modern, calm, focused, and audio-first. Avoid claiming that the app itself generates AI podcasts; its initial role is to play AI-generated podcast content.
